A showcase application that tracks satellites and orbital debris from NASA and NORAD data, and decomposes the gap between its prediction and independent observation into three measured terms: model error, element-set quantization, and floating-point arithmetic. Built on ktsu.PreciseNumber, ktsu.Semantics.Quantities and ktsu.ImGui.App, it compiles one propagator against four numeric storage types so the arithmetic contribution can be isolated and measured rather than assumed.
